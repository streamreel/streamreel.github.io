Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the StreamReel application ("the app", "we") accesses, how it is used and stored, and how access can be revoked. The app is a private tool used only by StreamReel to publish short video clips to StreamReel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and to monitor how those clips perform. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any account other than StreamReel's own.

Information the app accesses

ServiceWhat is accessedWhy
YouTube (Google) Permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and read-only access to our own channel (youtube.readonly): channel name and ID, and the processing status, privacy status and view counts of videos we uploaded. To upload our clips and confirm they processed correctly and were not rejected.
Instagram (Meta) Our own Instagram professional account's ID and username, permission to publish Reels, and the link and view counts of Reels we published. To publish our clips as Reels and confirm they are still live.
TikTok Our own TikTok account's basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) and permission to upload and publish videos, and the view counts of videos we published. To publish our clips to our TikTok account and track how they perform.

The app does not access other people's accounts, private messages, contacts, comments from other users, payment information or location data.

How the information is used

  • Only to publish StreamReel's own clips and to monitor those clips (status, removals and view counts).
  • It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to build profiles of anyone.
  • No data obtained through Google, Instagram or TikTok APIs is used to train artificial-intelligence models.

Before a clip is posted, frames from the clip and a transcript of its speech may be sent to our AI review provider (Anthropic) to check quality and compliance with platform rules. This is the public stream footage we are clipping. No account data from Google, Instagram or TikTok is included.

Google API Services

StreamReel's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

  • Access tokens are stored only on StreamReel's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server or shared.
  • Video status and view counts of our own clips are kept in a local database on that computer for our own performance reports, and are deleted if we stop using the platform.
  • If access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and we delete them.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites → remove the app.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove the app.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

The app and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
