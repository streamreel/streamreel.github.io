How it works

  • Permission first. We only clip creators who have given permission, through an official clipping campaign, a public invitation to clip, or a direct agreement.
  • Credit always. Every clip names the creator and links back to their channel and the full stream.
  • Checked before posting. Each clip is reviewed for quality, copyrighted music and platform rules before it is published.

The StreamReel app

StreamReel uses a small private application to upload finished clips to our own accounts and to check how they perform. It is used only by StreamReel to manage StreamReel's own channels; it is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts. See the privacy policy for exactly what data it uses.

Creators: if you'd like a clip of yours taken down, or want us to stop clipping you, email streamreel93@gmail.com and we'll remove it promptly.
